# Support

OpenJTD is a pre-stable, clean-room JTD/JTT/JTTC research and tooling project.
It is not a complete rendering engine or editor. The boundaries below keep
community help, security reports, and separately scoped paid engineering
distinct.

## Community help

Community help is best effort. For non-security questions, reproducible defect
reports, or contributions, use the repository's normal public collaboration
surfaces and follow [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md). Maintainers may not be
able to respond to every request or accept every contribution.

Do not post confidential, personal, proprietary, or redistribution-restricted
documents in public channels. Follow the sample-provenance requirements in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Security reports

Do not use community channels for suspected vulnerabilities. Follow
[SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for private reporting and coordinated disclosure.

## Separately scoped paid engineering

Paid engineering inquiries are handled only through [the maintainer's Upwork
profile](https://www.upwork.com/freelancers/eojinkim). Any engagement requires
an independent written scope and agreement. Availability, acceptance, and
deliverables are not promised by this repository or this document.

Commercial work must continue to respect the clean-room rules, third-party
rights, and sample-provenance requirements in
[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Boundaries

- Apache-2.0 does not create a paid-support entitlement, service-level
  agreement, response-time commitment, or obligation for any maintainer or
  contributor to perform work.
- OpenJTD does not promise production support, complete format compatibility,
  faithful layout rendering, native editing, security certification, or a
  particular commercial outcome.
- This page does not change the security-reporting process in
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) or the contribution and provenance rules in
  [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).
